# Changelog

## 1.2.2

- Games with 2+ monitors: Blip moves to your other screen and stays quiet there, then comes back after
  the game (setting: "During fullscreen games: Move to my other screen / Hide")
- Fix: browsers (Brave, Chrome, Edge, Firefox...) no longer make Blip disappear
- Hide Blip for 15 min / 1 hour / 3 hours / until you bring him back (right-click or tray menu, or say
  "hide for an hour" in the chat); he comes back with "I'm back!"
- No more spinning: the spin antic and spin dance move are gone, and the toy blaster now makes him
  skid backwards instead of spinning

## 1.2.1

- Fix: Blip now hides properly during games like Call of Duty (borderless fullscreen windows that span
  several monitors, games with hidden characters in their title, and Windows' own "game running" signal)
- Game buddy now recognises Call of Duty correctly
- New tray option: "Hide Blip for now"

## 1.2.0

- PC care (can be switched off): low disk space alert, weekly tidy-up (temp files + Recycle Bin, asks first),
  weekly startup-apps check (switch off what you don't need, reversible), game boost (High performance during
  games, switched back after), "restart helps" tip after 7 days, big RAM hog alert, weekly report
- Wellbeing nudges every hour (tea, water, stretch, eyes, posture...), adjustable, quiet at night/while gaming
- Achievements with a trophy shelf (16 to unlock, with bonus XP)
- Snack-catch mini-game (Play menu)
- Morning coffee mug and a nightcap when Blip sleeps at night
- Clipboard helper (off by default): copied colour codes and sums show in a bubble
- Evening summary of your day
- Fix: hide and seek no longer gives Blip away with a bubble over his head
- 9 new random antics: "oops, full auto" toy blaster, banana slip, bubble gum pop, giant dumbbell,
  paper plane that bonks him, balloon ride, hot chili fire breath, skateboard wipeout, magic trick gone wrong
- Power modes: say "power saving mode", "balanced" or "game mode" in the chat, or use the
  Power & night light menu (Windows' own power plans, no admin)
- Night light: Blip's own warm screen filter, on/off or on a schedule (default 21:00-07:00) with a warmth
  slider; works on every monitor and puts normal colours back when switched off or when Blip closes
- Hide and seek: more hiding spots (screen edges, peeking over the taskbar or over a window)
- Lighter: smaller install (93 MB), less memory, fewer frames when idle, RAM checks only when RAM is really full

## 1.1.0

- New glassy XP and happiness bars
- XP pop-up above Blip whenever you earn XP (name tag + mini bar + "+15 XP")
- More ways to earn XP: music/radio time, catching Blip mid-air, long throws, jumps, opening apps,
  first chat of the day, keeping Blip happy
- Daily quests (3 a day) and a weekly challenge
- Prestige Shop with Prestige Tokens: glasses, hats, capes/scarves/chains, trails, name-tag colours, emotes
- Updates now install automatically in the background (checks at start and every 6 hours;
  waits until you're not gaming, chatting or focusing). Can be switched off.

## 1.0.0 (first public release)

- See-through desktop pet that walks the taskbar, naps, sits on windows and climbs desktop icons
- Characters, colour themes, hats, seasonal outfits and 22 unlockable skins
- Levels 1-55 and 10 prestiges, with an aura and a badge for each prestige
- Dances to any music player; mini radio; Play/Pause/Next
- Chat: reminders, to-do list, focus timer, opening apps, game stats; optional local AI (Ollama)
- Random antics: football, NFL catch, hide and seek, sneeze, butterfly, spin, climbing
- Help my PC: RAM/CPU list, free RAM, close apps you tick, clean temp files, power plans
- Do Not Disturb, happiness meter, tiny sounds (off by default), petting, screenshot pose
- Installer (no admin), automatic updates (asks first), clean uninstall
